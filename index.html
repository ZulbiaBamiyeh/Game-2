How to Play Hokm

  • Teams. You & North are partners vs. West & East.
  • The Hakem. Each hand, one seat is dealt 5 cards first and becomes Hakem — they pick the trump suit (Hokm).
  • The Deal. Everyone then gets the rest, for 13 cards each.
  • Play. Follow the suit that was led if you can. If you can't, play any card — trumps beat everything else.
  • Tricks. Highest trump wins the trick, or the highest card of the led suit if no trump was played.
  • Winning a Hand. First team to win 7 tricks takes the hand. Win it 7-0 for a "Kot" — worth double.
  • Winning the Match. First team to 7 hand-points wins the match.
